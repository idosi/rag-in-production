# Month-End Close Procedure { #month-end-close-procedure }

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. A fictional internal procedure written for a portfolio project. It does not describe any real firm.

Owner: Senior Bookkeeper.

## Close calendar { #close-calendar }

The books for each client are closed by the 8th business day of the following month. Bank reconciliations are due by the 5th business day, and the reviewer signs off by the 8th.

## Bank reconciliation { #bank-reconciliation }

Reconcile every bank and credit card account to the statement balance. Any difference that cannot be explained is posted to the suspense account `9999` with a note, and the note says who is investigating it. An item may stay in suspense for at most one month; after that it goes to the reviewer.

## Reviewer sign-off { #reviewer-sign-off }

The reviewer checks the reconciliation, the suspense account and every journal entry above 10,000, then marks the month as closed in the accounting system. A closed month can be reopened only by a partner, and the reason is written in the close log.
