# Payment Approval Matrix { #payment-approval-matrix }

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. A fictional internal control written for a portfolio project. It does not describe any real firm.

Owner: Finance Lead. Applies to payments the firm makes from client accounts it manages, and to the firm's own supplier payments.

## Approval limits { #approval-limits }

| Payment amount | Who must approve |
|---|---|
| Up to 5,000 | the bookkeeper who owns the client |
| 5,001 to 50,000 | the bookkeeper and the reviewer |
| Above 50,000 | the reviewer and a partner |

Splitting one payment into smaller payments to stay under a limit is forbidden and is treated as a policy breach.

## New or changed bank details { #changed-bank-details }

A request to pay a new supplier, or to change a supplier's bank account, must be confirmed by a phone call to a number already on file, not to a number that appears in the request. Requests that arrive only by email are treated as possible fraud until confirmed. The confirmation is written in the payment note: who called, which number, and when.

## Urgent payments { #urgent-payments }

An urgent payment still needs the same approvals. Urgency is not a reason to skip a step, and a request that pushes for urgency together with new bank details must be reported to the Finance Lead.
