# Document Retention Policy { #document-retention-policy }

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. A fictional internal policy written for a portfolio project. It is not legal advice and does not describe any real firm.

Owner: Office Manager. Applies to every employee and contractor of the firm.

## Retention periods { #retention-periods }

| Record type | Keep for | Starts counting from |
|---|---|---|
| Client invoices, receipts and bank statements | 7 years | end of the client's tax year |
| Payroll records and payslips | 7 years | end of the calendar year |
| Signed engagement letters | 7 years | end of the engagement |
| Internal email that is not client work | 2 years | date sent |
| Job applications from candidates who were not hired | 6 months | date of the decision |

If a record belongs to more than one type, keep it for the longest period.

## Legal hold { #legal-hold }

When a partner places a client on legal hold (an audit, a dispute or a tax inquiry), nothing for that client may be deleted, even after its retention period ends. The hold is recorded in the client's folder as a file named `LEGAL-HOLD.txt`, and only the partner who placed it can lift it.

## Secure disposal { #secure-disposal }

Paper records are shredded with the cross-cut shredder in the archive room, never thrown in the recycling bin. Digital records are deleted from the document system and from the shared drive, and the Office Manager confirms the deletion in the disposal log every quarter.
