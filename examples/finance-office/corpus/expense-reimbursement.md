# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy { #expense-reimbursement-policy }

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. A fictional internal policy written for a portfolio project. It does not describe any real firm.

Owner: Finance Lead.

## What can be reimbursed { #what-can-be-reimbursed }

Travel to client sites, parking at client sites, professional course fees approved in advance by a partner, and office supplies bought when the office supply is out. Meals are reimbursed only when a client is present, up to 150 per person.

## How to submit a claim { #how-to-submit }

Submit the claim in the expense form within 30 days of the expense, with a photo of the receipt. Claims without a receipt are not paid. Mileage is claimed per kilometre at the rate published in the expense form, not as fuel receipts.

## Approval { #approval }

Claims up to 500 are approved by the employee's team lead. Claims above 500 need a partner's approval. Nobody approves their own claim.

## Payment { #payment }

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.
